    Why we celebrate Valaikappu

    Valaikappu is a blessing ceremony for the mother-to-be, held in the later months of pregnancy, often the fifth, seventh or ninth. The name comes from the Tamil words for bangle and protection, and the ceremony is centred on slipping colourful glass bangles onto her wrists. Some families also call it Seemantham.

    • A blessing for mother and baby

      Elders and loved ones bless the mother-to-be with turmeric, kumkum and sandal paste, and pray for her good health and a safe delivery.

    • The gentle sound of bangles

      It is believed that the soft jingle of the bangles reaches the baby and keeps the mother cheerful and calm.

    • Protection from the evil eye

      Bangles, flowers and holy marks are traditionally believed to guard mother and child from harm.

    • Care before the birth

      The whole family gathers to pamper her with her favourite foods, rest and love, so she feels supported as she prepares for motherhood.

    Above all, it is a way of telling her: you are not alone, and all of us are with you.
